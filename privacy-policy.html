Privacy Policy for GYW

Last Updated: December 30, 2024

Introduction

GYW ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account information (email, phone number, username)
  • Profile information (name, profile picture)
  • Messages and media you send through the app

Information Automatically Collected

  • Device information (device type, operating system)
  • Usage data (how you interact with the app)
  • Camera and microphone access (for video/audio calls and media sharing)

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain our messaging and calling services
  • Enable video and audio calls
  • Allow you to share photos and videos
  • Improve our services
  • Ensure app security and prevent fraud

Permissions We Request

Our app requests the following permissions:

  • Camera: To enable video calls and allow you to take photos/videos to share in messages
  • Microphone: To enable audio and video calls, and voice messages
  • Storage: To save and access photos/videos you share
  • Contacts: To help you find and connect with other users (optional)

Data Storage and Security

  • Messages are encrypted and stored securely
  • We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data
  • Your data is stored on secure servers

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Stream Chat: For messaging functionality
  • Stream Video: For video/audio calling
  • Clerk: For user authentication

These services have their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing them.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of certain data collection
  • Request a copy of your data

Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
